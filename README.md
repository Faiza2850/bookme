# BookMe (TAXIGO) 🚕

BookMe (TAXIGO) is a modern, comprehensive web platform designed to simplify airport taxi transfers. Whether you are arriving at or departing from any major UK airport, BookMe connects passengers with reliable, hassle-free transport solutions at the click of a button.

## ✨ Features

* **Instant Fare Quotes:** Input your startup pickup address and destination to check available routes and estimate fares instantly.
* **Diverse Vehicle Fleet:** Choose from an extensive selection of vehicles tailored to your needs:
  * **Saloon Car** (Up to 3 passengers, 3 suitcases)
  * **Executive Car** (Up to 3 passengers, 3 suitcases - premium comfort)
  * **Estate Car** (Up to 4 passengers, 4 suitcases)
  * **People Carrier / MPV** (Up to 5 passengers, 5 suitcases)
  * **Executive People Carrier** (Up to 5 passengers, 5 suitcases - premium)
  * **8-Seater Minibus** (Up to 8 passengers, 8 suitcases)
* **Interactive Live Chat:** Built-in real-time customer support platform to assist users instantly.
* **Comprehensive Portal:** Includes dedicated sections for Frequently Asked Questions (Q&A), detailed Terms & Conditions (T&C), and a direct Contact Form.
* **User Accounts:** Safe registration and login portal to manage your bookings, check history, and configure billing profiles.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

This platform is built using modern web technologies:

* **Frontend:** React.js / Next.js, HTML5, CSS3, Tailwind CSS
* **Routing & UI Components:** Responsive layouts, interactive modals, and dynamic state management.
